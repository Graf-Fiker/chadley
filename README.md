## Chadley – native Memos desktop client made with Textual + Python, coming soon

## Why Chadley?

I made this little app because I was pretty annoyed by Notesnook always
logging me out whenever I wanted to add an important note, and constantly
showing me advertising to buy their own sync feature. That's when I started
looking out for something I could self-host, and I found Memos.

I was kinda surprised how well it worked, but sadly there was no native
app. The web app is fine, but Chrome alone is chewing through like
800 MB – 1 GB of RAM on my system, and I thought: that's just too high.

Around the same time, I started loving TUI apps like Lazydocker more and more, and I
wanted to dip my toes further into Python. So I searched for a TUI toolkit
I could use, found out about "Textual" and honestly, it's pretty good.

And now here we are: Chadley got created.

## Features:

### Timeline and Notes

- **200 MB RAM USE** Lightweight and responsive, 
- Timeline-View and Memo Cards
- Write and ManageNotes in your Self hosted Memos instance
- Built-in calendar to browse and jump to notes by date
- Distraction-free focus mode for writing
- Three View Modes: Full height Cards | Expandable Cards | List View
- Filter your timeline with #Tags
- Checkbox support
- **Privacy first** no tracking or anything
- Fully keyboard-driven — but also Works with Mouse like a normal App
- Files Attachments **(Not ready yet)**
- German and English interface **(Not ready yet)**
